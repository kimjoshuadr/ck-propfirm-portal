# CK Propfirm

> Category: Brands

> Surface: web

*Transforming Traders into Winners globally.*

CK Propfirm provides proprietary trading evaluations and simulated funded accounts up to $1.2M with up to 100% profit splits, fast payouts, and 24/7 support.

## Voice & Tone

- **Adjectives:** confident, direct, trader-first, proof-led, plain-spoken
- **Tone:** Direct and benefit-led, never hypey. Leads with the offer — trade up to $1.2M in simulated capital and keep up to 100% of profits — then earns trust with verified payouts, Discord receipts and named trader stories. Urgency comes from scarcity (limited-time codes, 70% off), never fear. Speaks 'us vs us': community-first, anti-corporate, allergic to fine print. Numbers-forward, concrete, short declaratives; positions everything as a simulated evaluation, never real-money trading.

### Vocabulary
- **Use:** prop firm, evaluation, funded account, simulated environment, profit split, payout, drawdown, trading objectives, challenge, scaling ceiling, reward cycle, us vs us, no hidden fees, no time limits, news trading allowed
- **Avoid:** guaranteed profits, get rich quick, risk-free investing, real money trading, broker / brokerage / custodian, live accounts, passive income, world-class, cutting-edge, synergy

## Imagery

- **Style:** Dark premium and proof-first. Deep navy grounds lit by violet radial glows, with gold reserved for limited offers and CTAs. Authentic Discord payout receipts, verified payout certificates and trader success-story frames sit beside the brand's purple raccoon mascot.
- **Subjects:** payout proof screenshots and verified certificates, Discord community and named trader stories, the CK purple raccoon mascot, trading platforms (MetaTrader 5, TradeLocker), promo / limited-offer banners
- **Treatment:** Duotone dark navy (#030a1c–#080e24) lifted by violet (#703ad7 → #894cef) radial glow; gold gradient (#d49f3e → #fff494) only on offers and buttons. Screenshots are shown uncropped as evidence inside 12px-radius cards with 1px #26324a hairlines. Mascot art is isolated, square, and sits on the dark ground with a glow behind it.
- **Avoid:** stock photos of suited corporate traders, light or white backgrounds as the primary canvas, generic abstract gradients with no product or proof, real-money or live-market imagery — everything is simulated, redrawn brand marks or mascot substitutes

## Layout

- **Radius:** 12px
- **Border weight:** 1px
- **Spacing:** 4px scale · 8px rhythm

### Posture rules
- Dark-first: every page renders on the Void Navy canvas #030a1c with `html.dark`; light is the exception, not the default.
- Violet is the identity (#703ad7 → #894cef brand gradient, links, focus rings, glows); gold #d49f3e is the only CTA colour.
- Primary CTAs are fully-rounded gold-gradient pills with dark ink text #1a1030, an inset white highlight and a #d49f3e glow — never white-on-gold.
- Surfaces are glassy: #080e24 at ~75–92% opacity over the canvas (`--ck-glass` #080e24bf, `--ck-glass-strong` #080e24eb), 1px #26324a hairline, 12px radius (feature cards step up to 28px).
- One radial violet glow per section acts as the transition — it is the single flourish, never a page-wide wash.
- Social proof reads green: Trustpilot #00b67a for ratings, verified badges and payout totals.
- Metrics use tight display type (Plus Jakarta Sans 700–800) with muted #999ba3 labels; prices and payout amounts sit in mono.
- Left-aligned with generous vertical rhythm; one primary CTA per viewport and secondary actions are ghost/outline in violet.
